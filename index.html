PROGRESS THE END

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to the PTE iPhone app

The short version
  • You don't need an email or password. The app creates an anonymous account for you.
  • We use your data to run your training plan. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps.
  • Apple Health data is read on your phone and stays there. Only a score band (for example "green") leaves the phone.
  • The AI coach, usage analytics and sharing body details are all off until you turn them on.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app: Account ▸ Privacy & data ▸ Delete all my data.

2. What we collect and why

Your profile (from onboarding)

First name, position, level, height, weight, training days, equipment, season dates and phase, unit preference, timezone, your goals and what gets in your way, and the numbers you enter (lifts, sprint and jump results, target). We use them to build your program, size your fuel targets and show your progress.

How your body feels and your injury areas are sent to our server only if you agree to it during onboarding. You can withdraw that consent at any time in Account ▸ Privacy & data; we then delete those answers.

Your training

The sets you log, sessions you mark done, your "How hard was it?" rating, retest results and your daily body check-in. We store these so your history is kept and, if you use the coach, so it can see how training is going.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, PTE reads sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and active energy. It never writes to Health. Your recovery, readiness, strain and stress scores are calculated on your phone with fixed rules. The raw Health values are not sent to us; only today's readiness band (for example "green") is stored with your check-in. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

The AI coach (only if you turn it on)

When you turn on the coach, the messages you send it, the coach's replies, pain notes you report to it ("niggles"), changes it proposes and short notes it keeps about you are stored on our server. While the coach is on, your journal entries are stored too. To write a reply, our server sends the relevant parts of this data, plus your training data, to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model. The coach only suggests changes; your plan never changes unless you accept.

Some phrases (for example about self-harm or a serious injury) trigger a fixed, pre-written safety message instead of an AI reply.

Anonymous usage analytics (only if you opt in)

If you say yes, the app sends a small fixed set of events (for example "app opened", "tab viewed", "session marked done") with a random install ID to PostHog. These events are not linked to your account and never include health, pain, journal, Mind answers or anything you type. Location lookup from your IP address is switched off. You can turn this off at any time.

Things that stay on your phone

Your Body and Mind profiles, Mind conversation answers (unless you send one to the coach), your calendar (read on the phone to find games and practices), shopping lists, and voice dictation (recognised on the phone; no audio is recorded or sent). Sensitive data on the phone is kept in the iOS Keychain.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Providing the app (profile and training data): performance of our contract with you, Art. 6(1)(b).
  • Health-related data (body details, pain, check-ins, readiness band, coach conversations about your body or mind): your explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a).
  • AI coach and analytics: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a).
  • Keeping the service secure (rate limits, abuse prevention): our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f).

You can withdraw consent at any time in the app. This does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Coach messages: 30 days, then deleted automatically. A short summary of older conversation is kept while the coach is on.
  • Turning the coach off deletes your messages, the summary and your stored journal entries.
  • Coach usage counters: 2 days.
  • Everything else: until you delete your data or your account.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent.

  • Delete everything: Account ▸ Privacy & data ▸ Delete all my data. This deletes your account and all data on our server, then clears the app.
  • Delete coach data only: Account ▸ Privacy & data ▸ What my coach knows.
  • Anything else (a copy of your data, a correction): write to info@progresstheend.com.

Because accounts are anonymous, if you delete the app without using "Delete all my data" first, we cannot tell which server data is yours. Please delete your data in the app before removing it.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Belgium, the Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each athlete can only reach their own rows on our server. Keys for the AI provider are kept on the server, never in the app.

8. Health and safety

PTE adapts training. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose. If something hurts badly or you are struggling, talk to a doctor, physio or someone you trust.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
